Compressive Fourier-Domain Intensity Coupling (C-FOCUS) enables near-millimeter deep imaging in the intact mouse brain in vivo

Renzhi He, Yucheng Li, Brianna Urbina, Jiandi Wan, Yi Xue*,
University of California, Davis,
Paper arXiv Video Code Data

Abstract

Two-photon microscopy is a powerful tool for in vivo imaging, but its imaging depth is typically limited to a few hundred microns due to tissue scattering, even with existing scattering correction techniques. Moreover, most active scattering correction methods are restricted to small regions by the optical memory effect. Here, we introduce compressive Fourier-domain intensity coupling for scattering correction (C-FOCUS), an active scattering correction approach that integrates Fourier-domain intensity modulation with compressive sensing for two-photon microscopy. C-FOCUS enhances fluorescence intensity by over 20-fold in vivo, accelerates scattering correction by 5-fold compared to previous approaches, and enables multipatch correction across fields of view spanning hundreds of microns beyond the memory effect range. Using C-FOCUS, we demonstrate high-resolution imaging of YFP-labeled neurons and FITC-labeled blood vessels at depths exceeding 900 μm in the intact mouse brain in vivo. Furthermore, we achieve transcranial imaging of YFP-labeled dendritic structures through the intact adult mouse skull. C-FOCUS provides a broadly applicable strategy for rapid, deep-tissue optical imaging in the living brain.

Overview

C-FOCUS enables two-photon imaging of YFP-labeled pyramidal neurons up to 800 μm below the dura in the somatosensory cortex using intensity-based scattering correction with compressive sensing.

a, Optical schematic of the C-FOCUS system and comparison with adaptive optics (AO). b, an uncorrected image is first acquired and segmented into subregions, each containing a local intensity peak that serves as a correction target. c, Correction masks for the subregions are measured and calculated sequentially using compressive sensing. d, In vivo imaging of YFP-labeled pyramidal neurons in the somatosensory cortex of a Thy1-YFP-H mouse through a cranial window using 1035 nm excitation, with and without scattering correction. With C-FOCUS, fluorescence intensity is enhanced fivefold, enabling imaging to a depth of 800 μm (8.5 EAL).

Results

Fluorescent Beads through a Mouse Skull

Quantitative evaluation of C-FOCUS by imaging red fluorescent beads through a 250 μm mouse skull ex vivo.

a, Correction masks computed using 10–5,000 measurements and the corresponding corrected images. The fully sampled case uses 10,000 measurements. b, Image of the same fluorescent bead without correction (magnified 38-fold for display). c, Image of the bead using a low numerical aperture (NA) mask with size comparable to the DC component of the correction mask. d, Image using a correction mask generated by 2P-FOCUS with 10,000 measurements. e, Overlap of correction masks from 2P-FOCUS (cyan, 10,000 measurements) and C-FOCUS (green, 3,000 measurements; magenta, 5,000 measurements). f, Comparison of peak fluorescence intensity without correction (cyan), with low NA (green), with 2P-FOCUS (purple), and with C-FOCUS (magenta) using correction masks from a. g, Three-dimensional point spread functions measured through the skull using different binarization thresholds applied to the same grayscale correction mask, yielding different DMD output-to-input power ratios. h, Fluorescence intensity, i, image contrast, j, lateral ,and k, axial resolution at various power ratios. l, m, PSF cross-sections of the bead along the x- and z-axes, respectively. Line colors indicate power ratios from 7% to 43% with correction and 100% without correction, as shown in the colorbar.

Neurons

C-FOCUS enables in vivo visualization of pyramidal-neuron axons throughout the visual cortex at depths of up to 908 μm.

An adult Thy1-YFP-H mouse was imaged under anesthesia through a cranial window. a–c, Representative images without correction at depths from 32 to 652 μm. d, Volumetric renderings without correction (left) and with correction (right). e–g, Images without correction, with correction, and zoomed-in views at 780, 832, and 908 μm, respectively. Uncorrected-image intensities in the zoomed views are scaled by the indicated factors for visualization. h–j, Fluorescence intensity profiles along the dashed sampling lines in e–g: cyan dashed curves, without correction; magenta solid curves, with correction. k, Schematic of neuronal imaging through a cranial window. l, Correction subregions and corresponding masks at 816 μm. Matching numerical labels and colored outlines identify corresponding subregions and masks.

Blood Vessels

C-FOCUS enables in vivo visualization of FITC-labeled blood vessels at depths of up to 912 μm.

A wild-type mouse was imaged under anesthesia through a cranial window following intravenous injection of FITC. a–c, Representative images without correction at depths from 48 to 676 μm. d, Volumetric renderings of the 240 × 240 × 912 μm³ image stacks without correction (left) and with correction (right). e–g, Images without correction, with correction, and zoomed-in views at 756, 800, and 908 μm, respectively. Uncorrected-image intensities in the zoomed views are scaled by the indicated factors for visualization. h–j, Fluorescence intensity profiles along the dashed sampling lines in e–g: cyan dashed curves, without correction; magenta solid curves, with correction. k–m, Correction subregions and representative masks corresponding to the boxed regions. Matching numerical labels and colored outlines identify corresponding subregions and masks.

Neurons through Intact Skull

C-FOCUS visualizes the apical dendrites of YFP-labeled pyramidal neurons through 110 μm-thick intact skull in vivo.

a, The volume view of the skull (second-harmonic signal (SHG)) and YFP-labeled dendrites without and with correction. The left column shows the representative SHG image at 50 μm depth (1.1 EAL) and YFP images at 170 μm (3.2 EAL) and 220 μm (3.4 EAL) depth without correction. b-d, Comparison between the images without correct (left column) and the images with correction (middle column), and zoomed-in views (right column, scale bar, 10 μm) at b 430 μm (3.9 EAL), c 490 μm (4.2 EAL), and d 560 μm (4.5 EAL) depth. In the zoomed-in views, the intensity of images without correction are magnified (as labeled in the images) for display purpose. e-g, Fluorescence intensity profiles along the dashed sampling lines in the zoomed-in views. Cyan dashed curves, without correction; magenta solid curves, with correction. h-j, Subregions and correction masks on the same z-plane as b–d. Matching numerical labels and colored outlines identify corresponding subregions and masks. k, Schematic of in vivo transcranial imaging of YFP neurons through an intact skull. The coverglass is to protect the exposed skull after survive surgery.
